How trials work

  1. Fill in the Join Club form.
  2. Check the trial date in the Events table.
  3. Arrive 15 minutes early with your college ID.
  4. Play the trial session with the coach.
  5. Results are announced within a week.

Trial requirements

  • Valid college ID card
  • Sports shoes and comfortable clothes
  • A water bottle
